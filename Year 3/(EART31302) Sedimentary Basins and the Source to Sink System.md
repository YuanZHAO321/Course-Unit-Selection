Course unit overview
This module covers the origin and nature of sedimentary basins, their stratigraphic fill, sequence stratigraphy and considers the entire sediment source-to-sink sedimentary routing in a range of settings

Aims
1. To develop a detailed understanding of how sedimentary basins form, the different types of such basins and the expression and characteristics of these sedimentary basins.

 

 

2. To understand how the stratigraphic record within sedimentary basins is formed and preserved and how it responds to intrinsic variability of depositional systems and varying tectonic and climatic processes.

 

 

3. To understand the seismic-scale expression of sedimentary basins and their sedimentary fill.

Teaching and learning methods
The unit is taught via lectures with practical exercises. Exercises use real-world oil field problems and serve to apply theoretical knowledge into practice. The contents are assessed via an examination and independent coursework, which requires students to apply knowledge to an unknown problem, research key information themselves, and integrate into a field development program. Informal feedback is available during lectures and practical exercises. Written feedback will be provided with marked coursework. The exam scripts can be viewed at the start of semester 2.
