## Course unit overview

The module examines the supply of energy to meet global demand. After an introduction to global energy usage and resources, this applied geology unit reviews the fundamentals of the main energy sources (oil and gas, geothermal nuclear and renewables (wind and solar)). The methods used for exploration and exploitation of hydrocarbons, and uranium resources are discussed, together with the increasing importance of renewable energy generation and its role in the energy mix. Carbon capture and storage is examined as a potential way of sequestering CO2 to meet global climate change mitigation commitments.

Following an overview of oil and gas exploration and production, and a review of the petroleum system students are given typical subsurface lithological and geophysical data, and in a series of practical exercises are introduced to methods to evaluate the information and interpret the results.

The unit also briefly introduces the exploration for Geotherrmal energy, looking at ongoing activites and future potential of deep hot rocks and low temperature geothermal.  Wind Energy is reviewed, discussing the techniques used and analysis of the shallow subsurface.

Uranium mining and processing, and the use of uranium in the civil nuclear power industry is discussed.

## Aims

The course introduces students to world energy usage and the extraction of resources to meet global demand. We evaluate fossil fuels, nuclear and renewable energy sources.

## Learning outcomes

On the successful completion of the course, students will be able to:

- **ILO 1** — Summarize current global energy usage and describe how that energy is sourced
- **ILO 2** — Compare the role of renewable energy in the energy mix and assess its economic viability; evaluate the role of carbon capture and storage in the energy transition
- **ILO 3** — Discuss the genesis, maturation and accumulation of hydrocarbon, analyse some typical subsurface geological data and interpret the results to evaluate the presence of oil and gas and assess the potential volume trapped in the subsurface
- **ILO 4** — Describe the techniques and interpretation of data used to evaluate the subsurface, including wireline logs, cores, cuttings
- **ILO 5** — Describe methods for Carbon Capture and Storage (CCS) as a method to mitigate climate change.
- **ILO 6** — Describe the methodologies to capture Geothermal Energy, current exploration and subsurface analysis and future potential
- **ILO 7** — Describe the methods used to evaluate the shallow subsurface for siting of wind turbines and associated facilities
- **ILO 8** — Outline the methods of exploration, evaluation and exploitation of uranium and its usage in the nuclear energy industry

## Syllabus

- Week 1: Global Energy Demand and Supply and role of fossil fuels
- Week 2: Hydrocarbon Reservoir Characterisation
- Week 3: Drilling and Well Data Analysis
- Week 4: Source Rocks and Geochemistry
- Week 5: Reservoir characterisation
- Week 6: Wireline Logs and Basic Petrophysics
- Week 7: Calculating Hydrocarbon Resources in a Prospect
- Week 8: Carbon Capture and Storage - A solution to the carbon problem?
- Week 9: Geothermal Energy
- Week 10: Wind Energy
- Week 11: Nuclear energy and the nuclear fuel cycle

## Teaching and learning methods
