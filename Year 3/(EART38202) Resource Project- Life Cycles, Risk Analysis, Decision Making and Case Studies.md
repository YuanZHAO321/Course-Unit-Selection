## Course unit overview

The resource industry is often involved in carrying out large-scale and cost-intensive projects that need to deal with a range of subsurface, socio-economic, and political uncertainties. Professionals selecting, commissioning or executing such resource projects need to be aware of a range of issues in order to make robust objective decisions. This unit will illustrate these concepts using examples of constraints, strategies and technologies via representative case studies from the petroleum, minerals, aggregates or water sectors. The unit will integrate knowledge of mineral deposits, petroleum geology, geophysics, remote sensing, structural geology, and introduces students to project evaluation, risk analysis, and the environmental and human impacts of resource projects. Students will learn about professional roles and job descriptions of geologists within the resource industry.

## Aims

To develop an integrated view of the management, decision-making processes and technologies, and constraints of typical resource projects via representative case studies.

## Learning outcomes

On the successful completion of the course, students will be able to:

Developed

Assessed

- **ILO 1** — Identify and discuss geological, environmental, economic and political constraints on resource project viability. — Y, Y
- **ILO 2** — Build a data acquisition program for resource identification, evaluation, extraction and processing/beneficiation (e.g. remote sensing, analog studies, geochemistry, geophysics, drilling), in keeping with geological, environmental, socio-economic and political constraints. — Y, Y

- **ILO 6** — Communicate scientific findings clearly and succinctly to an audience of academics. — Y, Y

## Syllabus

- Week 1: Introduction to resource projects, value chain
- Week 2: Project management and economics
- Week 3: Hydrocarbons: Exploration
- Week 4: Hydrocarbons: Appraisal
- Week 5: Hydrocarbons: Production
- Week 6: Mineral deposits: Exploration
- Week 7: Mineral deposits: Appraisal
- Week 8: Mineral deposits: Production
- Week 9: Uncertainty, geological and statistical models in resource definition and economics
- Week 10: Legacy of resource projects, footprint of resource projects, environmental regulations

## Teaching and learning methods

Delivered around several case studies, with introductory lectures on project life cycle, resource and project economics, subsurface uncertainties, and project constraints. Majority of unit consists of guided tutorials and practicals.
